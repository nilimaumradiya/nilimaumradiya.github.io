Executive Summary

Systemic Winter Surges and Severe Localized Contamination

This exploratory data analysis reveals significant geospatial and seasonal variances in phthalate contamination across Jabalpur. The visualization model relies on calculating aggregated median concentrations—grouping by chemical compound, geographical area, and seasonality—to isolate severe outliers and establish contamination baselines.

Critical Finding: The analysis discovered an extreme localized contamination event in the Ramnagra area (median 120 vs ~0 in Bhongdwar, Lalpur, and Ranjhi), coupled with a systemic, city-wide increase in chemical concentrations during the winter season (reaching an 11.3× amplification in drinking water).

Technical Methodology & Engineering

Technical Methodology & Data Modeling Pipeline

To produce clean, industry-standard visualizations and actionable business intelligence, the underlying data pipeline deployed rigorous dimensional modeling and statistical aggregation logic.

Non-Parametric Aggregation: Median vs. Mean

In environmental data with massive point-source spikes (like Ramnagra's 38.37 DnBP anomaly), arithmetic means severely distort baselines. If means had been calculated, neighboring regions like Bhongdwar and Lalpur would have artificially registered false contamination alerts (~30 µg/L) due to geographic boundary bleeding. Deploying median calculations isolated the true zero baselines of surrounding regions while highlighting Ramnagra as an isolated point-source crisis.

-- SQL Dimensional Aggregation Query WITH MedianCalculations AS ( SELECT g.area_name, c.compound_name, d.season, PERCENTILE_CONT(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY f.concentration_val) OVER (PARTITION BY g.area_name, c.compound_name, d.season) AS median_concentration FROM Fact_Water_Quality_Samples f INNER JOIN Dim_Geography g ON f.geo_id = g.geo_id INNER JOIN Dim_Chemical c ON f.chemical_id = c.chemical_id INNER JOIN Dim_Date d ON f.date_id = d.date_id ) SELECT DISTINCT area_name, compound_name, season, median_concentration FROM MedianCalculations;

Policy & Public Health Directives

Strategic Civic & Infrastructural Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analytics and statistical modeling, the following targeted interventions are submitted to municipal water authorities and healthcare policymakers:

01

Urgent Ramnagra Industrial & Conduit Audit

Immediate targeted chemical sampling must be deployed across Ramnagra’s feeder pipelines to locate specific industrial point-sources of DnBP (38.37) and DEHP (12.69), isolating leaking polymer distribution pipes or illicit manufacturing effluents.

02

Winter Civic Water Treatment Adjustment

Municipal water treatment protocols must adopt dynamic seasonal chemical filtration. Dosage rates of activated carbon or advanced oxidation processes must increase during winter to mitigate the documented 11.3× seasonal concentration amplification.

03

Real-Time Sensor Deployment & Continuous BI Surveillance

Transition from periodic manual batch testing to an IoT-driven telemetry pipeline feeding directly into the Power BI risk dashboard, with automatic threshold triggers firing whenever phthalate totals exceed 5.0 µg/L.
